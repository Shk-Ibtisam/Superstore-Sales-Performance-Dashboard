Superstore Sales Performance Dashboard — Power BI

A multi-page Power BI dashboard analyzing Superstore's sales performance across 2019-2020, covering KPIs, YoY trends, regional performance, and a 15-day sales forecast.

Built KPI cards, YoY comparison charts, a geographic sales map, and category/sub-category breakdowns using Power Query and DAX
Found the Consumer segment drives 48% of total sales, ahead of Corporate (33%) and Home Office (19%)
Uncovered that Cash on Delivery is still the leading payment method (43%), ahead of Online (35%) and Card payments
Identified a strong Q4 surge in 2020, with both sales and profit climbing sharply from September through December compared to 2019
Found Office Supplies leads all categories (644K), with Phones as the top sub-category (197K)
Discovered California, New York, and Texas are the top-performing states, with California alone contributing 335K in sales — nearly double New York's 187K
Found Standard Class shipping dominates (912K), showing customers prioritize cost over delivery speed
Built a 15-day sales forecast, revealing volatile daily demand through Q4 and a projected slowdown heading into January 2021

Tech: Power BI · DAX · Power Query · Forecasting
